Cord works as a PWA on iOS and Android.

## Installing

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS (Safari)">
    1. Open [runcord.com](https://runcord.com) in Safari
    2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android (Chrome)">
    1. Open [runcord.com](https://runcord.com) in Chrome
    2. Tap **three-dot menu** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Install**
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Navigation

Bottom sheet navigation. Tap a session to open it, switch between chat/changes/terminal with tabs.

## Voice input

Tap the mic icon to dictate. Toggle **Autosend after voice dictation** in Settings to send immediately without review.

## What you can do on mobile

- View session list with status indicators
- Read diffs with syntax highlighting
- Send follow-up messages
- Quick PR, Create PR, merge

## Slack from mobile

With the [Slack integration](/integrations/slack), tag `@Cord` from your phone's Slack app to start agents.
