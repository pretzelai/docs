## AI costs

Cord doesn't charge for AI usage. You bring your own subscription or API keys:

- **Claude Max/Pro subscription** -- use your existing Anthropic plan
- **Codex subscription** -- use your existing OpenAI plan
- **API keys** -- bring keys from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or other providers

You pay the AI provider directly unless you are using Cord-provisioned credits. Cord charges for the platform and compute according to your workspace plan.
