## Free

**\$0** -- for trying things out.

- 25 sandbox hours
- Up to 3 repositories
- \$100 in free AI credits
- No credit card required

The Free plan is a good way to evaluate Cord on a real project before committing.

## Pro

**\$100 per developer / month** -- for teams shipping with agents daily.

- 250 sandbox hours per developer
- Unlimited repositories
- [Slack integration](/integrations/slack)
- [Linear integration](/integrations/linear)
- Priority support

No commitment -- cancel anytime. Billed monthly per seat.

## AI costs

Cord doesn't charge for AI usage. You bring your own subscription or API keys:

- **Claude Max/Pro subscription** -- use your existing Anthropic plan
- **Codex subscription** -- use your existing OpenAI plan
- **API keys** -- bring keys from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or other providers

You pay the AI provider directly. Cord only charges for the platform and compute.
