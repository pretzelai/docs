## What are sandbox hours?

A sandbox hour is one hour of active compute time in a cloud sandbox. This is the primary unit of usage on Cord.

## What counts

- **Active compute** -- time when a sandbox is running (agent working, dev server active, you browsing the preview)
- **Startup time** -- the few seconds it takes to spin up a new sandbox

## What doesn't count

- **Idle/suspended time** -- when a sandbox is suspended, you're not charged
- **Resume time** -- waking up a suspended sandbox is near-instant and not billed separately
- **Cord UI usage** -- browsing sessions, reviewing diffs, and reading chat history is free

## How suspension works

Sandboxes automatically suspend after a period of inactivity. When suspended:

- No compute is billed
- File changes and state are preserved
- The sandbox resumes instantly when you return

This means you don't need to worry about forgetting to close a session. Idle sandboxes cost nothing.

## Checking your usage

View your current usage in **Settings > Usage**. You can see:

- Total sandbox hours used this billing period
- Hours remaining on your plan
- Per-session breakdown

## Overages

On the Free plan, when you've used your 25 hours, you'll need to upgrade to Pro to continue.

On the Pro plan (250 hours per developer per month), additional hours are available at a per-hour rate. Contact support for details on overage pricing.

Enterprise plans have unlimited sandbox hours.
